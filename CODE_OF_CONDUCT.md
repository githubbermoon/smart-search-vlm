# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for contributors and users of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's data.
- Give credit for work and preserve authorship.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, impersonation, plagiarism, and sustained disruption are not tolerated. Publishing private user content or security details without permission is also unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community and project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on the [maintainer profile](https://github.com/githubbermoon). Do not use a public issue when a report contains sensitive personal information. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, reviews, and project-affiliated spaces.
